Privacy Policy — Angkor Poster

Angkor Poster uses TikTok Login to connect your account.

We may store: TikTok user ID / open_id, access token and refresh token, video files you choose to publish, and captions you write.

We use this data only to publish your videos to your authorized TikTok account.

We do not sell your data. We do not collect passwords.

You can disconnect the app in TikTok settings at any time.
